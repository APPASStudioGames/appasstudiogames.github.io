Description of the Service

Saps is a mobile app available on Google Play Store (Android) and Apple App Store (iOS) that offers a different curious fact every day in areas such as science, history, etymology, astronomy, biology, and other disciplines.

Users can rate each fact with a star system, save them to their personal library, and view global community ratings. Additionally, the complete fact history can be accessed through the calendar.

Free Plan and Pro Plan

Free Plan
Allows access to today's curious fact, rating it, and saving it to your personal library. Access to previous days' facts not previously rated requires watching a rewarded ad or having the Pro Plan.

Pro Plan · One-time Payment
The Pro Plan is acquired through a single payment. The price is always shown in the app and in the store before you confirm the purchase, and it may vary by region. The Pro Plan grants:
• Full and unlimited access to the calendar history.
• Elimination of rewarded ads to unlock content.

The Pro Plan is not a subscription. There are no automatic renewals or recurring charges. The purchase is processed through Google Play (Android) or Apple App Store (iOS), depending on the platform.

Purchase Restoration
The Pro Plan is linked to your Google Play or Apple ID account. If you uninstall the app or change devices, the Pro Plan can be automatically restored when launching the app as long as you keep the same account.

Refunds
Digital purchases are governed by the refund policy of Google Play or Apple App Store, depending on where the purchase was made. On Google Play, requests made within 48 hours of purchase are handled automatically by Google; after that period, requests may be reviewed and resolved by APPAS Studio Games through Google Play Console. On the Apple App Store, refunds are handled exclusively by Apple via reportaproblem.apple.com. If you have any problem with a purchase, you can write to us at appas.studiogames@gmail.com.
