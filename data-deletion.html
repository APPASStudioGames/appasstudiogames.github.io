Data Deletion

Last updated: June 2026 · Saps

01

About this page

This page explains how to request the deletion of data associated with Saps, an app developed and operated by APPAS Studio Games. Saps does not require registration or a user account, and we do not collect names, email addresses or any directly identifying information. The data we hold is linked only to an anonymous device identifier.

02

How to request deletion

There are two ways to delete the data associated with your device:

Option A — From within the app (recommended)

  1. Open Saps and go to Settings.
  2. Tap "Delete my data".
  3. Confirm. The app — which knows its own anonymous device identifier — immediately removes all data associated with your device from our servers and clears the data stored locally on the device.

Option B — By email

  1. Send an email to appas.studiogames@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request – Saps".
  2. State that you want all data associated with your device removed. We will process the request and confirm by email once completed.

Additionally, uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data from your device (theme, language, unlocked dates, Pro status) and stops any further data collection.

03

Data that is deleted

Upon a valid request, we delete the following data associated with your device identifier from our servers:

• The anonymous device identifier.
• Your ratings of curious facts.
• Any fact proposals you have submitted.
• Your unlocked calendar dates.

04

Data that is retained

We do not retain any personally identifiable data, because none is collected. Aggregated and fully anonymized statistics that cannot be linked back to any device may be retained for the operation of the global ranking. Transaction records held by the app stores (Google Play / Apple) and by our purchase processor (RevenueCat) are governed by their own retention policies and may be kept where required for legal or accounting purposes.

05

Timeframe

Deletion requests are processed within 30 days of receipt. There are no additional retention periods beyond those described above for legally required records.
