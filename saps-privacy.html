Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026 · Saps

01

Data Controller

Saps is an app developed and operated by APPAS Studio Games. For any privacy-related inquiries, contact us at:

appas.studiogames@gmail.com

02

Data We Collect

Saps does not require registration or a user account. We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any other personally identifiable data. The data we process includes:

a) Anonymous Device Identifier
We generate a unique random identifier stored locally on your device. This ID contains no personal information and is used exclusively to associate your ratings anonymously on our servers. It does not allow us to identify you personally.

b) Ratings
The scores you give to curious facts are stored locally and, anonymously, on our servers to display global ratings.

c) Fact Proposals
If you submit a proposal, the text and category are stored on our servers. They are not linked to any personal data.

d) Local Preferences
The visual theme (light/dark), selected language, unlocked dates, and Pro Plan status are stored exclusively on your device via local storage (AsyncStorage). They are not sent to any server.

e) Advertising Identifier (GAID / IDFA)
The app displays rewarded ads through Google AdMob. Google may access your device's advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID on Android, IDFA on iOS) to personalize ads. You can reset or disable this identifier from your device settings.

03

Purpose of Processing

Data is used exclusively for:

• Displaying the global ranking of most-rated facts.
• Reviewing and publishing user-submitted fact proposals.
• Unlocking content through rewarded ads.
• Validating and restoring the purchased Pro Plan.
• Improving the app experience.

We do not sell, transfer, or share your data with third parties for their own commercial purposes.

04

Third-Party Services

Saps integrates the following third-party services, each with its own privacy policy:

Supabase (supabase.com)
Stores anonymous ratings and fact proposals. Privacy policy: supabase.com/privacy

RevenueCat (revenuecat.com)
Manages Pro Plan purchase validation. RevenueCat receives transaction information from Google Play or Apple App Store to confirm the purchase. Privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy

Google AdMob (Google LLC)
Displays rewarded ads in the app. Google may collect and use your device's advertising identifier to show relevant ads. Privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy
To disable personalized ads: device settings → Google → Ads.

Google Play / Apple App Store
App distribution and Pro Plan payment processing are handled through Google Play (Android) and Apple App Store (iOS), subject to their own privacy policies.

05

Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), data processing is based on:

• Legitimate interest: for the operation of the anonymous global ranking and service improvement.
• Contract execution: for the validation and restoration of the purchased Pro Plan.
• Consent: for the use of the advertising identifier for ad personalization purposes.

06

Data Retention

• Local data on your device is retained until you uninstall the app.
• Anonymous ratings on our servers are retained while the app is in operation.
• Fact proposals are deleted once reviewed and processed.

Although the data is anonymous, it is associated with a device identifier, so the data linked to your device can be located and deleted. You can do this from within the app under Settings → "Delete my data", or by emailing us at appas.studiogames@gmail.com. Requests are processed within 30 days. See our Data Deletion page for details.

07

Your Rights

If you are a resident of the EEA, you have the right to:

• Access the data we hold about you.
• Request correction of inaccurate data.
• Request deletion of your data.
• Object to the processing of your data.
• File a complaint with the supervisory authority (in Spain: Agencia Española de Protección de Datos, aepd.es).

To exercise these rights, contact us at: appas.studiogames@gmail.com

08

Minors

Saps is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA under applicable legislation). We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If we detect that we have collected data from a minor without verifiable parental consent, we will delete it immediately.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal data, contact us at: appas.studiogames@gmail.com

09

International Transfers

The third-party services used (Supabase, RevenueCat, Google) may transfer data to servers located outside the EEA. These transfers are carried out under the appropriate guarantees required by the GDPR (such as the Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission).

10

Security

We implement adequate technical and organizational measures to protect data against unauthorized access, loss, or destruction. All data transmitted to our servers is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS.

11

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. When we do, we will update the date at the top. We will notify you through an app update in case of significant changes. Continued use of the app after publication of the changes implies acceptance of the new policy.
